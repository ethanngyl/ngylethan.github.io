3D object scanner

Walk around an object with your phone, and get a navigable 3D model back. Photos go to a server-side reconstruction pipeline built on COLMAP and Gaussian Splatting, and the result renders in our own engine instead of an off-the-shelf viewer.

I lead five CS students and two UX designers, own the API that ties the mobile capture app to the reconstruction backend, and build the engine sandbox and visualiser the splats land in.

COLMAPGaussian Splatting.plyC++REST APIMobile
  1. CaptureMobile app guides photos from many angles
  2. IngestUpload service queues the image set
  3. ReconstructCOLMAP camera poses, then Gaussian Splatting
  4. RenderCustom engine draws the splats and .ply point clouds
  5. InspectSandbox UI to orbit, crop and compare scans
